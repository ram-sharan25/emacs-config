* Workflow guideline for future automation
This section documents the complete weekly-review responsibility. It is not
copied into captured reviews. Keep the recurring review within ten minutes:
automation should gather and summarize evidence, while the person reviews,
judges, and decides.

** Automation principles
- Never silently reschedule, cancel, complete, or create commitments.
- Present links back to source entries so every summary can be verified.
- Highlight exceptions and changes instead of reproducing every task.
- Prefer one review dashboard generated before the ritual begins.
- Require confirmation before applying proposed task or calendar changes.
- Preserve each written review as a dated historical record.

** Get Clear
*** Automate or assist
- Collect counts and links for unprocessed GTD, phone, notes, email, and other known inboxes.
- Identify inbox items older than an agreed threshold.
- Offer one consolidated list of possible loose ends.
*** Human responsibility
- Notice uncaptured promises, worries, ideas, and obligations.
- Decide whether each item is actionable, reference, someday, or trash.

** Get Current
*** Automate or assist
- Summarize tasks completed during the week.
- List scheduled or deadline items that were not completed.
- Show the previous and upcoming week from the calendar.
- List Waiting For items with age and last follow-up date.
- Flag active projects with no next action, no recent activity, or stale deadlines.
- Flag stale Next Actions and newly relevant Someday/Maybe items.
*** Human responsibility
- Confirm whether the system reflects reality.
- Choose do, defer, delegate, drop, or renegotiate.
- Decide which projects and opportunities still matter.

** Reflect
*** Automate or assist
- Provide factual signals: completions, carryovers, focus time, habits, and notable calendar events.
- Compare with recent weeks to reveal recurring postponement or overload.
*** Human responsibility
- Name the week's win, friction, lesson, energy, and emotional meaning.
- Interpret patterns without treating metrics as judgment.

** Choose the next week
*** Automate or assist
- Show existing commitments, available time, deadlines, and unresolved carryovers.
- Detect obvious schedule conflicts and unrealistic workload.
- Suggest source-linked candidate outcomes without selecting them automatically.
*** Human responsibility
- Select one primary and at most two supporting outcomes.
- Decide what not to prioritize.
- Define Monday's first visible action.

** Close
*** Automate or assist
- Convert confirmed decisions into proposed task, calendar, Waiting For, or project updates.
- Validate that chosen outcomes have concrete next actions.
- Save and link the dated review.
*** Human responsibility
- Approve changes and mark the ritual complete.
